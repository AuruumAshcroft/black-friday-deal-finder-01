How these deals were checked

Every deal on this page was found and checked by the maker on 27 November 2026, reading the price at the named store or in a deals roundup that names the store. Each card shows when its price was checked and where it was read; the detail view (click any card) links to that source.

Verdicts mean:

  • New low — the lowest price reported for that product so far.
  • Strong deal — a genuinely good price, well below usual.
  • Routine sale — a normal sale. Fine to buy, nothing special.
  • Inflated — the “usual” price is padded, so the cut looks bigger than it is. None of the deals on this page carry this label.

Prices change during Black Friday week — the store link on each card takes you to the product at that store so you can confirm the price before you buy. Streaming deals show a monthly price.
